About Maharashtra Fire & Emergency Services

Maharashtra Fire & Emergency Services is committed to safeguarding lives, property and the environment through timely emergency response, effective fire prevention measures and continuous public awareness. Over the years, the department has evolved into a modern emergency response organization equipped with trained personnel, specialized firefighting equipment and advanced rescue capabilities. The department responds to a wide range of emergencies including fire incidents, road accidents, building collapses, industrial disasters, flood rescue operations, hazardous material incidents and other emergencies where immediate assistance is required. Along with emergency response, the department actively promotes fire safety awareness through educational campaigns, inspections, safety audits and training programs for industries, institutions and the general public. Through continuous modernization, technological advancement and professional training, Maharashtra Fire & Emergency Services strives to provide efficient, reliable and citizen-centric emergency services while ensuring the highest standards of safety and preparedness across the state.
