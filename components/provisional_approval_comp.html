Provisional Fire Approval

Applications for obtaining Provisional Fire Approval can be submitted online through the E-Approval Portal . Please read the instructions carefully before applying.

Documents Required for Obtaining Fire Approval / Provisional No-Objection Certificate

  1. Duly filled application form in prescribed format.
  2. Approved building plan issued by the competent authority.
  3. Ownership documents or valid lease agreement.
  4. Fire protection system layout drawing.
  5. Any additional document required by the department.

Application for Final No Objection Certificate

  1. Fire protection completion certificate.
  2. Testing reports of installed fire safety systems.
  3. Compliance certificate from licensed agency.
  4. Request letter for final inspection.
